Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about FileLens.

How much does FileLens cost?

FileLens costs ₹199 as a one-time payment for a lifetime license. This includes all features, 1 year of free updates, and email support. There are no subscriptions or recurring fees.

Is there a free trial?

Yes! FileLens offers a 7-day free trial with all features fully unlocked. No credit card needed — just download and start using. After 7 days, you'll need to purchase a license key to continue.

What operating systems does FileLens support?

FileLens runs on Windows 10 and Windows 11 (64-bit). It requires approximately 150MB of disk space including OCR. It works on both Intel and AMD processors.

Can FileLens search inside images and scanned PDFs?

Yes! FileLens uses OCR to extract text from images (PNG, JPG, BMP, GIF, WebP, TIFF) and scanned PDFs. It supports both English and Hindi OCR, so you can search text inside images in either language.

How do I purchase a license key?

Pay ₹199 via UPI (scan the QR code on the purchase page), take a screenshot of the payment, and email it to filelenspro@gmail.com with your name and email. You'll receive your serial key within 24 hours. Then open FileLens → Settings → License → Enter key → Activate.

Does FileLens work offline?

Yes! FileLens is 100% offline. It doesn't require an internet connection for search, indexing, or OCR. The only time it needs internet is for the Google transliteration feature in the Hindi keyboard (but offline transliteration is also available as a fallback).

Can I index cloud drives like Google Drive?

Yes! FileLens can index Google Drive, OneDrive, and Dropbox folders. It also supports network drives (NAS, SMB shares). Simply add the folder path in Settings → Indexing → Add Folder.

Is my data private and secure?

Absolutely. FileLens stores all index data locally on your machine in a SQLite database. No data is sent to any server. The license validation is also completely offline — no internet connection needed after activation.
