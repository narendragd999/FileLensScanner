Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the FileLens mobile application handles your data. Your privacy and security are our top priorities.

The Short Version

FileLens operates entirely offline. We do not collect, transmit, store, or share your personal data, scanned documents, or files on any external servers. Everything happens 100% locally on your device.

Permissions We Request

To provide its core functionality, FileLens requires the following device permissions:

  • Camera: Required to take pictures of documents, receipts, and cards so that text can be extracted (OCR) and saved locally.
  • Storage (Photos & Media): Required to import existing photos for text extraction, save your scanned documents, and maintain the local SQLite database that powers the high-speed search feature.

How Your Data is Handled

When you scan a document or allow FileLens to index your files, the text extraction (OCR) and indexing processes are performed by the processor on your phone.

  • No Cloud Uploads: Your documents are never uploaded to a cloud server for processing.
  • No Tracking: We do not use third-party analytics trackers to monitor your behavior.
  • Total Control: All indexed data is stored in a secure database inside the app's local sandbox. If you uninstall the app, all indexed data and tags are permanently deleted from your device.

Optional AI Features

If you choose to use optional generative AI features (like multimodal AI document summaries), those specific features may require sending the text or image you explicitly select to the AI provider (e.g., Google Gemini) for processing. You will always be prompted to accept the AI terms and conditions before this happens, and it is strictly opt-in.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Since we do not collect your email address, we cannot notify you directly of changes. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer via the support channels listed on our Google Play Store page.
